# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

---

# Product Name

**PyReplay Trader**

---

# 1. Vision

Build a professional-grade Bar Replay engine similar to TradingView that allows:

* Step-by-step market simulation
* Manual and automated strategy backtesting
* Realistic trade execution
* Multi-asset and multi-timeframe replay

---

# 2. Core Features

## 2.1 Replay Engine

* Play
* Pause
* Step forward (1 bar)
* Step backward (1 bar)
* Jump to date
* Adjustable replay speed

---

## 2.2 Trading Simulation

* Buy / Sell (Market)
* Close position
* Modify SL / TP
* Simulated spreads
* Commission support
* Account balance tracking
* Margin calculation

---

## 2.3.1 Indicators

* SMA
* EMA
* RSI
* MACD
* Bollinger Bands
* Custom indicator injection

## 2.3.2 Drawings

* Trend lines
* Horizontal lines
* Vertical lines
* Fibonacci retracement
* Text annotations
* Arrow annotations

---

## 2.4 Strategy Backtesting

* Attach Python strategy
* Run auto mode
* Display:

  * Win rate
  * Profit factor
  * Drawdown
  * Sharpe ratio
  * Equity curve

---

## 2.5 Multi Asset Support

* Major FX pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY)
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* Indices (if broker supports)

---

## 2.6 Multi Timeframe Support

* M1
* M5
* M15
* H1
* H4
* D1

---

## 2.7 UI Controls

* Chart zoom
* Indicator toggle
* Trade history overlay
* Equity curve panel
